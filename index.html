Setup

Drag the box on the preview to move it. Keep the LEDs small in frame and leave something bright in shot — iOS gives no manual exposure control to a web page, so whole-frame metering is the only lever you have over clipping. Watch the clip figure: above about 1% the LED core is white in all three channels and only its bloom is classifiable.

Detector

Scores are the mean per-pixel excess of one channel over the brighter of the other two, across the ROI. A clipped pixel contributes nothing to either, by construction.
